# SAMPAAA — 3-minute demo script

## Before recording

- Open the [live app](https://sampaaa-130242409804.asia-south1.run.app/) and confirm the map loads.
- Pre-run **New layout on a seasonal wetland**, **Established high ground**, and one Locality Lens report in separate tabs so the three-minute demo never waits on Earth Engine.
- Return the main tab to the landing page. Keep a Bengaluru address ready in the search field for the boundary segment.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

**On screen:** Landing page and globe.

**Say:**

> Buying land is often the biggest decision a family makes, but the site visit only shows what is there today. It does not show that a plot held seasonal water for decades, flooded during a past monsoon, or was recently filled and built over. SAMPAAA turns that hidden physical history into evidence a buyer can understand before paying a deposit.

## 0:20–1:10 — Seasonal wetland example

**On screen:** Open the prepared **New layout on a seasonal wetland** report.

**Do:**

1. Pause on the headline and overall risk.
2. Scroll through **What stands out**: water history, radar-confirmed flood and low terrain.
3. Point to **Checked and clear** and **What changed here**.
4. Open **Evidence** briefly and toggle a water or flood layer on the map.

**Say:**

> The Overview leads with this plot's most important signals instead of a generic dashboard. Here, decades of water observations, radar flooding and drainage-relative elevation rise to the top. Every sentence cites the exact Earth Engine evidence behind it. Gemini investigates and explains, but transparent rules set the baseline rating and a verifier rejects unsupported claims or numbers. The full Evidence tab keeps the time series, sources and map layers available for scrutiny.

## 1:10–1:40 — Basavanagudi contrast

**On screen:** Switch to the prepared **Established high ground · Basavanagudi** report.

**Say:**

> The same pipeline does not manufacture a dramatic story everywhere. On this established high-ground example, the report surfaces the strongest available signal and keeps the clear checks compact. That contrast is important: SAMPAAA is not a red-flag generator; it is a consistent evidence reader.

**Do:** Point to the lower-risk headline, a clear item and the land-health evidence or NDVI/LST layers.

## 1:40–2:15 — Draw the real plot

**On screen:** Return home, search the prepared Bengaluru address, and choose **Draw / import**.

**Do:**

1. Click four corners on the map and close the polygon.
2. Show live square-metre, acre, gunta and perimeter measurements.
3. Point to **Import KML or GeoJSON** without opening the picker.

**Say:**

> A circular buffer is useful for exploration, but land decisions are parcel-shaped. I can draw the boundary directly or import the buyer's KML or GeoJSON. SAMPAAA validates the polygon and then clips every Earth Engine query and map layer to this exact shape. The measurements update immediately, and this button runs the same verified report for the custom boundary.

## 2:15–2:45 — Locality with sources

**On screen:** Open the prepared Locality Lens report.

**Do:**

1. Show amenity markers and the deterministic liveability score.
2. Open **Community Voices** and show the source links / Google Search grounding entry.

**Say:**

> Physical risk is only half the decision. Locality Lens uses Google Places for transit, groceries, healthcare, schools, food and parks. The score is a transparent distance-and-count formula—not an AI opinion. Gemini separately summarises what residents say using Google Search grounding, shows the source pages, and keeps those opinions out of the score.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close

**On screen:** Return to the wetland headline or a split view of the report and map.

**Say:**

> SAMPAAA does not replace title checks, engineers or a monsoon site visit. It gives buyers the questions they did not know to ask, backed by decades of Google Earth Engine data and verified Gemini reasoning. Before you buy land, see what it used to be.
